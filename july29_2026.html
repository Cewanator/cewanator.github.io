was it important?

i haven't done much creatively since the pseudoregalia vid. to be honest, i don't know how i feel about it. not in terms of quality, and i recognize that it isn't some kind of citizen kane-esc pièce de résistance, but i mean just on a personal note:

was it worth it?

for something that was bouncing in my head for a year, that i was compelled to make, why does the video feel so inconsequential now? from a purely skill development standpoint, i could see an arguement for it being important, but from a cultural perspective, was it ever important? i'm compelled to say my piece on this after watching the Dan Olsen video I Don't Know James Rolfe, which is a super fascinating video but it always leads me to a point of reflection. one of the thesis of that vid proposes that, like the AVGN, Dan is insecure about being a "filmmaker" and is simply a "youtuber" instead. this leads me to ponder what i've done in the creative space. i don't know what qualifies as "art". i don't know what pieces have "value". what makes a movie a film? what elevates a game to the status of art? what's the difference between an introspective documentary video vs a hand drawn animation vs a goofy video game review? is one more important than the other?

i feel i must confess that i don't believe that the videos i've made are art. i don't think im pretentious enough to pretend that lebron james scream if you love btd6 is art. i'm very conflicted to argue that any review can be consider art (perhaps a debate for another time) but i still wonder; do these endevors have value? intrensic or extrensic? if so, why?

even taking a step further back, does this blog post have value? can you consider the thoughts of a goofball on the internet art?
maybe it's all just what you make of it.

now aside from all that nonsense, things are going well for me :) no real plans for creating anything rn but i liked this rant. been toying with the idea of doing "games in games" again but we'll see if i commit to that or not. music has been fun tho, would also like to do more recordings there. lowkey just been busy enjoying the social bits of life.

keep it breezy, see you at the next one.
